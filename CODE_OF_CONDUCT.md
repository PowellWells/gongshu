# Gongshu Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Gongshu community respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Welcome questions and contributions from newcomers.
- Give credit for work, data, ideas, and third-party material.
- Disclose uncertainty, limitations, conflicts of interest, and safety risks.
- Respect research, privacy, security, licensing, and publication boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading capability, benchmark, authorship, or provenance
  claims.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or abuse of project communication channels.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing the Gongshu project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact the repository
maintainers through an available private channel on GitHub. If a private
maintainer channel is unavailable or the concern involves platform abuse, use
GitHub's reporting and support mechanisms.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as
privately and promptly as practical, with attention to the safety of everyone
involved.
